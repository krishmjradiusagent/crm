6a · alternative

Everything in one card

The address, rename pencil and status dropdown move inside as the card's own top row, and the grey gutter goes: checklist, facts, controls and photo become ruled sections of a single white card instead of cards inside a tray. The top row carries one flat left-to-right gradient in Radius indigo, from #ececfe to #f7f7ff — two hex stops a developer can swap in a line, no blur, no second hue. The address ink drops to a near-black indigo. Back arrow and status pill unchanged.
